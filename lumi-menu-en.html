Why LUMINA-30?

LUMINA-30 is about preserving a practical human ability to refuse, pause, verify, or redirect before an AI-related path becomes effectively irreversible.

This does not require assuming that catastrophe is certain. It requires taking seriously one narrower question: while choices still exist, can a human “NO” still work in practice?

Why LUMINA-30?

We do not need to settle today whether advanced AI will cause catastrophe, stop short of it, or develop along a different path.

If the danger becomes severe, humans need a real way to stop. If the danger can be avoided, humans need the authority, time, and information to redirect before the opportunity disappears.

Either way, the same boundary matters: before escalation becomes irreversible, human refusal, stopping, verification, and redirection should still be practically effective.

LUMINA-30 is an independent public reference framework built around that boundary. It is not a certification system, a safety guarantee, an adopted standard, or a substitute for human judgment.

What does this have to do with you?

This is not a promise that LUMINA-30 can secure anyone’s future. The practical concern is simpler: will you, people you care about, and future generations still have meaningful room to question, refuse, pause, verify, or redirect consequential AI-driven decisions before options disappear?

Preserving that room is valuable whether you are optimistic, uncertain, or worried about advanced AI.

How can you engage with LUMINA-30?

New to the issue

Understand the boundary, decide whether it matters to you, and share it if you think others should see it.

Using or deploying AI

Use the One-Question Pilot and practical review tools where real decisions, approvals, incidents, or escalations occur.

Developing or researching AI

Test the boundary, criticize weak claims, compare it with existing control and safety practices, and identify where refusal is only formal.

Governance, audit, policy, procurement

Use the framework as a non-binding review lens alongside existing law, standards, controls, and human accountability.
